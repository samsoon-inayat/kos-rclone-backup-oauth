Privacy Policy

kos-rclone-backup is a personal, non-commercial OAuth application used only by its owner for encrypted backups from a private Ubuntu server to the owner's Google Drive.

The application is not offered to the public and does not sell, share, or disclose Google user data to third parties.

Google authorization data is used only to permit the owner's rclone installation to access the owner's Google Drive for the backup workflow. OAuth credentials and tokens are stored on the owner's private server and are not stored in this public website or repository.

Backup contents are controlled by the owner. The intended workflow stores backup archives in encrypted form before or while they are transferred to Google Drive.

If access is no longer desired, the owner can revoke the application's Google account authorization and remove the local rclone credentials.
